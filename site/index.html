Everything about your quotas, nothing else

Your accounts at a glance

A ring for the active account, a bar for the others, and the age of every reading. Inactive accounts keep their last value; once their reset time passes they show as “probably refilled”.

Told which account to switch to

When the account you use is at 10 % or less, alerts and the tray tooltip name another account of the same tool with at least 30 % left and 20 points more. It never switches accounts for you.

Details and history

Each account keeps up to 90 days of local readings. See its remaining quota over 24 hours or 7 days, its banked resets, its subscription period and a cautious estimate of when it runs out at the recent pace.

Reset calendar and banked credits

Upcoming resets day by day, plus your Codex reset credits with their expiry. Get notified when a new credit arrives, and advised to use one before it expires unused.

Google Calendar and Gmail, in one click

Connect Google Calendar and every banked reset lands in your calendar with reminders, removed once you use it. An email from your own Gmail the day before a credit expires.

Readable at any taskbar size

The percentage and its thin gauge are drawn at the exact pixel size of your taskbar icon, light or dark. Hover it for a preview, and click a quota there to show it in the icon. Using both tools? Show one icon per tool.

For coding agents

A local MCP server, off by default, lets your coding agent read quotas, plan around resets and change settings, with local confirmation for anything external. The same commands work on the command line, with JSON output.

  • Which accounts still have weekly quota left? Say how old the data is.
  • Which resets are coming in the next three days?
  • Turn on Windows reminders one hour before weekly resets.

Frequently asked questions

Something else? Ask on GitHub.

How do I see how much Codex usage I have left?

Install Codex Tracker on Windows and keep using Codex as usual. The tray icon shows the remaining percentage of the active account; hover it for the 5-hour and weekly limits and the next reset time.

When does my Codex weekly limit reset?

Each Codex account has its own reset time. Codex Tracker reads it from Codex itself and shows the exact date and hour for every account, in a list or a week view, with an optional reminder.

Does it work with Claude Code?

Yes. The Claude desktop app is detected automatically. For Claude Code in a terminal, copy the status line setting offered in Settings → General → Claude Code.

What are Codex reset credits?

Codex can grant banked reset credits that refill a limit on demand and expire after a while. Codex Tracker lists them with their expiry, notifies you when a new one arrives, can add it to Google Calendar and advises using one before it is wasted.

Why is only the open account updated?

The tracker reads the active account in Codex and in Claude. Other accounts keep their last dated reading until you open them again.

Does it need to stay open?

For reminders, yes: it sits quietly in the taskbar and can start with Windows. The PC must be on.

Is Codex Tracker free and private?

Yes. It is free and open source (MIT). There is no sign-up and no server: it reads the quotas Codex and Claude already know on your PC, never your conversations or passwords. Only optional SMS, calls and SendGrid emails go through your own provider accounts and their prices.

Is it affiliated with OpenAI or Anthropic?

No. Codex Tracker is an independent project. Codex and Claude are trademarks of their respective owners.
